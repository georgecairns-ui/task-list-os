# Step 1: check your setup

Goal: know what you can reach, and what the person is working with, before you connect anything.

## 1.1 Check you can work in this folder

Quietly, without asking:

1. Read `setup/progress.md`.
2. Write a test line to the "Notes for next time" section of `setup/progress.md`, read it back, then remove it.

If either fails, stop and ask them to give you access to the folder (see START-HERE.md). Do not carry on.

## 1.2 Look at what you already have

Quietly, without asking:

1. **Your own tools.** Look at the tools and connections available to you right now. Note any for email (for example Gmail or Outlook), calendar, cloud files (Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox), accounting, CRM or chat. Note whether you can run code or commands on their computer.
2. **Where this folder lives.** Work out its location from its path. Is it in Downloads or on the Desktop? Inside a cloud-synced folder (Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, iCloud)? A shared team folder?
3. **What's around it.** Look one level up. Is there an existing business folder structure, a to-do list, or anything describing the business? Note it; step 4 and step 5 use it.

## 1.3 Ask about their setup

Ask these in one short message, numbered. Skip any you already know from 1.2 and say so.

1. Are you on a Mac or a Windows computer?
2. Do you have Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge? (The task list app needs one of them. Both are free.)
3. Which email do you use for the business? For example Gmail or Google Workspace, Outlook or Microsoft 365, or something else.
4. Which calendar do you use? (Usually the same company as your email.)
5. Is it just you, or do other people work in the business with you?

If they ask why: "So I connect the right things and don't waste your time on the wrong ones."

## 1.4 Sort out the folder's home

- **In Downloads or on the Desktop:** suggest moving it somewhere permanent, such as Documents, before going further. The task list app remembers where the folder is, so moving it later means reconnecting. If you can move it yourself, offer to. If not, tell them how in one or two plain steps, then ask them to give you access to its new location.
- **In a cloud-synced folder:** good, it's backed up. Tell them so. If it's a folder shared with other people, point out that anyone with access can read their task list and business notes, and ask if that's what they want.
- **Renaming:** they can rename the folder to anything they like (for example "Fern & Finch HQ"). Suggest doing it now if they want to, for the same reason as moving it.

## 1.5 Write it down

Fill in the "Your setup" section at the top of `context/tools.md`:

- Computer: Mac or Windows
- Browser for the task list: Chrome or Edge
- Claude: which version they use (the desktop app, the website, or Claude Code), if you can tell
- Workspace location: the folder path, and whether it is cloud-synced or shared
- Email provider, calendar provider
- Solo or team (and roughly how many people)

Tick step 1 in `setup/progress.md`.

## What to say

> Thanks. You're on a Mac with Chrome, your email and calendar are Google, and it's just you for now. Next I'll connect your email and calendar, so I can spot new work as it comes in. I'll only ever read and draft. Nothing gets sent without you.
